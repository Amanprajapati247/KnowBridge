# Round 3 Commitments & Roadmap
**Tech Titan | KnowBridge – AI-Powered Project Knowledge Handover Platform**

As required by Round 2 Hackathon guidelines, the following are our **three substantial, committed improvements** scheduled for delivery in Round 3:

---

### Commitment 1: Enterprise Ecosystem Integrations (GitHub, Task Boards & Document Platforms)
- **Deep Integration with Repositories:** Direct GitHub/GitLab webhooks and API connectors to ingest pull request discussions, commit summaries, and architecture decision records (ADRs).
- **Task Board Synchronization:** Two-way sync with Jira, Linear, and Trello to track closed vs. in-flight sprints, blocking bugs, and assignee handoffs in real-time.
- **Cloud Document Storage:** Live connectors to Google Docs, Notion, and Confluence with automatic delta tracking to trigger handover updates when source specs change.

---

### Commitment 2: Advanced Role-Specific Personas & Multilingual Handovers
- **Targeted Persona Views:** Rather than a single incoming-member view, generate tailored handover views for different incoming profiles:
  - *Engineering Lead View:* Deep dive into technical debt, architecture, CI/CD, and open PRs.
  - *Product/Club Coordinator View:* Focus on stakeholders, budget, vendor deadlines, and event milestones.
  - *QA & Operations View:* Known defects, test coverage gaps, and release runbooks.
- **Multilingual Support & Audio Walkthroughs:** Automatic translation of approved handovers into regional languages and synthetic audio briefing generation for accessible onboarding.

---

### Commitment 3: Handover Version Diffing, Formal Sign-Off & Follow-Up Pulse
- **Visual Diff Engine:** Interactive side-by-side diffing between Handover Version 1.0, 1.1, and 2.0 to show what has changed since the previous team member departed.
- **Formal Sign-off & Acknowledgement Protocol:** Cryptographically verifiable read-and-accept acknowledgement signed by the incoming member, logging comprehension checks.
- **Automated 14-Day & 30-Day Check-in Pulse:** Scheduled automated nudges to the incoming member to check if any undocumented blockers arose, feeding back into the project's institutional memory.
